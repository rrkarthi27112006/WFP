How It Works

1. Assign

Teachers create tasks — homework, projects, quizzes and more — for a class or a single student, with due dates and marks.

2. Prioritize & Submit

Students see what to work on first, track deadlines, and submit text, files or images straight from their dashboard.

3. Review & Track

Teachers mark and give feedback; students and parents watch progress build up over the term.

For Students

One dashboard for every subject: pending, completed, and overdue work, a completion rate, and a plain-language answer to "what should I work on first?"

For Teachers

Assign work to a class or an individual student, see who hasn't submitted, review and grade in one place, and post announcements without leaving EduTrack.

Smart Priority

EduTrack weighs due date, difficulty, estimated effort and marks to tell each student which task genuinely deserves their attention first — and explains why in one sentence.

Academic Progress

Subject-wise completion, workload analysis, and early warnings for missed deadlines keep small problems from becoming big ones.
